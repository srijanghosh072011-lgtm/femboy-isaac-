Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be home for the appointment?
Not for exterior-only work — just leave gates unlocked and we'll take care of the rest. For interior cleaning we'll arrange a time that works for you.
How much does window cleaning cost in Regina?
Most single-family homes land between roughly $149 and $349 depending on the number of windows and whether you'd like interior, exterior, or eavestroughs added. Your written quote is always free and fixed before we begin.
Are you insured?
Yes — Prairie Shine carries full liability insurance and WCB coverage, so you and your property are protected on every visit.
What if I'm not happy with the results?
Then we're not done. Our streak-free guarantee means if you spot anything, we return and make it right at no extra cost.
Do you clean windows in winter?
We're busiest spring through fall, but we take interior work and select exterior jobs year-round when Saskatchewan weather allows. Just ask and we'll let you know.
