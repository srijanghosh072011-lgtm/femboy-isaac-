Good to know

Questions I get a lot

How much does window cleaning cost in Regina?
For most Regina homes it lands somewhere between $149 for outside-only and around $300 for inside and out with the screens and tracks done. It really comes down to how many windows you've got and whether it's one or two storeys. Either way, I'll give you a firm price before I start — no surprises.
Do you clean the inside of the windows too?
Yep. Most folks book inside and out together — that's when you really notice the difference. I lay down cloths, keep my shoes off, and leave your sills cleaner than I found them.
Do I need to be home?
Not for outside-only — just leave the gate unlocked and I'll take care of it. If you'd like the insides done, we'll pick a time that suits you.
Are you insured?
I am — full liability and WCB coverage. So if anything ever went wrong on the job, it's on me, not you.
What if I'm not happy with a window?
Then I'm not finished. Point it out and I'll come back and redo it, no charge. I'd rather you tell me than call someone else next time.
