Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

Template notice: This is a general-information starting point drafted to align with Canada's PIPEDA. It is not legal advice — please have it reviewed and completed with Isaac's real business details (legal name, contact person, and tools used) before publishing.

1. Who we are

Viewshine Window Cleaning ("we", "us", "our") is a residential window cleaning business based in Regina, Saskatchewan. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. What information we collect

We only collect what we need to prepare a quote and provide our services, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your service address and details about your property (e.g. number of windows, storeys)
  • Messages you send us through our contact or quote form
  • Basic, non-identifying website analytics (such as pages visited), if you accept analytics cookies

3. Why we collect it

We use your personal information to: respond to your enquiry, prepare and deliver a quote, schedule and complete your service, send you an invoice, and follow up about your service. We do not sell your information to anyone.

4. Consent

By submitting our form or contacting us, you consent to us using your information for the purposes above. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (subject to legal or contractual limits).

5. Cookies & analytics

Our site may use cookies to understand general traffic patterns. Non-essential (analytics/marketing) cookies are off by default — you choose whether to accept them through our cookie banner. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings.

6. How we protect your information

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from loss, theft, and unauthorized access.

7. Access & corrections

You have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to request its deletion. Contact us and we'll respond as required under PIPEDA.

8. Email & CASL

If you opt in to receive reminders or offers by email, every message will identify us, include our contact details, and provide a working unsubscribe link that we honour promptly, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).
