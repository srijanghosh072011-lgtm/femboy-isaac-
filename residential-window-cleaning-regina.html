How much does house window cleaning cost in Regina?
Most Regina homes come in between about $149 for outside-only and around $300 for inside and out with the screens and tracks done. It comes down to how many windows you've got and whether it's one or two storeys — and you'll always have a firm price before I lift a squeegee.
Do you do the screens and tracks?
Always — they come with the inside-and-out clean. I wash the screens by hand and vacuum out the tracks, because clean glass in a gritty track only looks half-done.
Do I have to be home?
Not for outside-only — just leave the gate open. If you want the insides done, we'll line up a time that suits you.
