Common eavestrough questions

How often should eavestroughs be cleaned in Regina?
At least once a year — ideally in fall once the leaves have dropped, and again in spring if you have a lot of nearby trees. Regular clearing prevents leaks, foundation problems and winter ice dams.
Do you clean up the debris?
Always. We remove debris by hand, bag it and take it away, then flush your downspouts and confirm everything drains properly before we leave.
Can you do windows and eavestroughs together?
Yes — our Shine Bundle combines a full window clean and eavestrough clear-out in one visit, which saves you money and a second appointment.
